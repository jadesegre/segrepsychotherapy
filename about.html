ABOUT

Jade Segre is a psychodynamic therapist practicing virtually and in person in New York City. She is currently training at the Greene Clinic in addition to working at a private practice. She works with individuals and couples and has specialties in gender and sexuality, life transitions, relationship issues and indifidelity, college students, and working with the parents of trans kids.

Individuals ▼

In individual work Jade helps patients identify the relational patterns in their life, like those of conflict, disconnection, loneliness, and fear. Through interrogating cycles from the past and their manifestations in the present, Jade helps patients feel more connected, not only to those around them, but more importantly to themselves. Individual work will help build a bridge to new ways of living and being, to new possibility. We can meet once or twice weekly, whatever feels appropriate for each individual person.

Couples ▼

Couples work is dynamic and challenging, it asks partners to witness each other.
