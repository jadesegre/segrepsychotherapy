FEES

My full fee for individual therapy is $200 and for couples is $225. I offer a sliding scale which allows those with means to subsidize the treatment of those who otherwise could not afford treatment.

Sliding scale guidelines

Consider paying higher on the scale if you:

  • Have disposable income to spend on vacations and luxuries
  • Own your home or live rent free
  • Have inherited or will inherit wealth
  • Have private higher education or advanced degrees
  • Your income is over $200,000 or make enough from investments that work is optional
  • Have out of network insurance benefits (see below)

Consider paying lower on the scale if you:

  • Live paycheck-to-paycheck or are unable to save
  • Have lower earning potential due to lack of higher education
  • Do not have financial support from your family
  • Lack stable housing or consistent insome
  • Struggle to meet your needs on a day-to-day basis

Out of Network Benefits

Your insurance may offer out of network reimbursement. Generally, with out of network benefits you will be reimbursed for 40-70% of the cost of treatment. I am happy to provide superbills at the end of each month which you can submit for reimbursement. If you have questions about using insurance, don't hesitate to contact me
